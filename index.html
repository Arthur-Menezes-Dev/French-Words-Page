Common Words

Let's take a looks at some of the most used words in french language, daily Spoken by natives.

Common Verbs

verb is a word that expresses an action, an occurrence, or a state of being. Every complete sentence needs at least one verb to tell what the subject is doing or experiencing (such as run, happen, or exist).

Common Adverbs

An adverb is a word that describes or modifies a verb, an adjective, or another adverb. It typically tells how, when, where, or how much something happens.

Common Nouns

A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, or idea. Common examples include dog, city, apple, and happiness.

Common Adjectives

An adjective is a word that describes or modifies a noun or pronoun. It adds details like size, color, or feeling (for example, happy, blue, or tall).
